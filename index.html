Camp Purpose

"Lift where you stand" is our call to action this summer. We will focus on growing in our current roles, serving those around us, and strengthening our connection to the Savior at Camp Grady Spruce.

Camp Essentials

Everything you need to know before we head to the lake.

Clothing & Gear
  • Sleeping bag and pillow
  • Modest swimwear & sunblock
  • 5 days of active clothes
  • Sturdy hiking shoes
  • Refillable water bottle

Coordinated by Ward Leaders:

  • Ward flags and spirit gear
  • Extra water jugs and coolers
  • First-aid kit (campsite)
  • Canopy or shade structures

  1. No swimming/boating without lifeguards.
  2. Live the "For the Strength of Youth" standards.
  3. Follow "Leave No Trace" principles.
  4. Encouraged "digital fast" for camp activities.

Cell Service? Limited signal at Possum Kingdom Lake.

Food? Meals served in the dining hall; report allergies at registration.
